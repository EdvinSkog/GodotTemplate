Props are scenes that have no (or very minimal) impact on gameplay (such as collision).

Some basic examples are:
	* A coffe mug
	* A table (with collisions set up)
	* A tree (with some swaying animation)

However, some props may have a bit of functionality (scripts, animations)
	* A traffic light (of which the different lights can be toggled)
	* A ceiling fan (that consistently spins, emits audio, includes an on/off toggle function)

It's a technical definition, so things you might not ordinarily refer to as a prop, can be considered one;
This includes larger concepts:
	* A skyscraper (used in the background of a scene)
	* Roads
	* Terrain models
